Disposition store

What makes a character read as independent is not surprise but consistency under novel conditions. This accumulates only what emerged — nothing is entered in advance, because deciding her traits is authoring them.

Audio machine

Many takes of the same short phrase, layered, offset, detuned and scattered across the field — an underdetermined substrate rather than a hidden signal. The interesting zone is usually just barely underdetermined: chorus, not crowd.

Files stay on your disk. They are read in the browser and cached in this page's own storage. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is written into the repository — which is the point, if the voices are not yours to redistribute.

If the dialog greys your file out, switch its filter to All files — the type filter depends on what your desktop thinks the file is, not on what the app can read.

They speak over each other. One page can only produce one voice at a time, because speechSynthesis is a single queue — but that queue belongs to the browsing context, not to the machine. Each layer therefore runs in its own hidden frame with its own queue and its own voice, and the engine underneath mixes them. Overlap test counts how many are actually sounding at once, so this is checkable rather than claimed.

A production substrate, never an evidence source. Anything marked while this is running is filed in a separate stream and never mixes with the main ledger. Pareidolia dissolves once you understand its mechanism; by that test most of what this produces is a symptom by construction.
