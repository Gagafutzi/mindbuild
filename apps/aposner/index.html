aPOSNER

Paced auditory arithmetic with a four-way answer cross

  1. A voice says a digit (1–9) at a steady pace. The first one: just remember it.
  2. With each new digit, the centre tile shows + or −. Combine the new digit with the digit you heard just before it — not with your previous answer.
  3. Pick the right answer among the four tiles: tap/click it, or use the arrow keys (WASD / ZQSD work too). No answer before the next digit counts as wrong.

This browser has no speech synthesis. Turn on “Show the digit on screen” in Settings to practise visually.
