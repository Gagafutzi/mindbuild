Loci Span

A list of words, one at a time. Walk a route you know and set each word, as a picture, at the next place along it. Then walk the route again and write them down in order.

Imagery

Session

Adapting, a list grows by one word after a perfect list and shrinks by one after two misses in a row, and the next session starts where this one ended. In any order, a list is perfect when every word comes back, wherever it is written.

Walk the route

Type each word in order and press Enter. Enter on an empty line skips a place; Backspace on an empty line takes the last one back.
